# Portfolio structure

~~~text
Portfolio/
├── index.html
├── Resume.pdf
├── README.md
├── STRUCTURE.md
├── assets/
│   ├── favicons/
│   └── images/
│       ├── faizan.webp
│       └── img.png
├── css/
│   └── styles.css
├── js/
│   └── script.js
└── output/
    └── playwright/
~~~

## Responsibilities

- index.html: semantic content, SEO metadata, structured data, and inline SVGs
- css/styles.css: design tokens, layouts, responsive behavior, and motion policy
- js/script.js: accessible navigation, progressive reveals, active section state,
  scroll progress, and dynamic year
- Resume.pdf: current downloadable résumé
- assets/images/faizan.webp: optimized primary portrait
- assets/images/img.png: original portrait fallback

The site is intentionally framework-free and requires no build step.
